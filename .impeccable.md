## Design Context

### Users

Prospective clients reviewing MM.S work across desktop and mobile, often moving quickly through visual project rivers while testing the site controls. The experience must remain equally usable with touch, trackpad, mouse, and keyboard.

### Brand Personality

Editorial, raw, playful, and precise. MM.S should feel like an intentionally direct personal studio site rather than a polished generic agency template.

### Aesthetic Direction

Keep the established plain-HTML character, asymmetric responsive grid, varied media proportions, and deliberately simple native-looking controls. The control panel is a playful design tool, while typography, spacing, and interaction geometry must remain rigorously aligned. Avoid decorative UI conventions that dilute this identity.

### Design Principles

- Preserve the raw editorial identity while refining optical alignment and responsiveness.
- Keep layout behavior width-driven and interaction behavior input-aware.
- Separate visual artwork size from accessible touch-target size.
- Use hard, intentional startup cuts with no crossfades or smooth interpolation.
- Verify every Cargo draft change after a complete editor reload and on real iOS Safari when browser chrome is involved.
