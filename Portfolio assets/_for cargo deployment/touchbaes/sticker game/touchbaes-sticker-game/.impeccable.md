## Design Context

### Users
Portfolio visitors viewing the Touchbaes sticker-room interaction on desktop and mobile. They are not trying to complete a complex game; they are briefly playing with a raw, tactile brand moment.

### Brand Personality
Raw, playful, tactile.

### Aesthetic Direction
Transparent page with no decorative background, no framed app shell, and no instructional UI. The scene and sticker assets should carry the experience. The tweezer interaction should feel like a physical object picking up paper stickers.

### Design Principles
- Preserve the desktop composition wherever possible.
- Keep all additions visually quiet and asset-led.
- Make mobile touch playable without turning the piece into a conventional mobile app.
- Keep original source assets intact; use optimized delivery assets for hosting.
- Avoid explanatory text unless it is part of the tweezer interaction feedback.
