## Phase 1 — alpha video rebuild (item 1)
- Pipeline change: webm --(ffmpeg libvpx-vp9 decode, keep straight
  alpha)--> ProRes 4444 .mov --(Apple avconvert
  PresetHEVCHighestQualityWithAlpha)--> HEVC-alpha .mp4. Apple encoder =
  Apple playback semantics; removes ffmpeg's videotoolbox alpha quirks.
- Verify: AVFoundation containsAlphaChannel=1 AND visual: Safari over
  magenta/green test page, zoomed edge screenshot — no white halo, bg
  shows through. Compare against the current file side by side.
- Upload both (8bit + hf) via input#file, swap the data-mp4 attrs.
