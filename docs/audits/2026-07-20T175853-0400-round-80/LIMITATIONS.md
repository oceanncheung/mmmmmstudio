# Phase 1 capture limitations

- Figma's full-file and full Home-page traversal exceeded the bridge request window. The approved Home desktop/mobile nodes, their screenshots, the variables, components, and the other relevant page inventories were captured; the unavailable full Home tree is not represented as complete.
- Cargo's read-only API exposes saved server state, not text that may exist only in an unsaved editor input. The captured saved draft matched the published primary artifacts at capture time.
- Cargo returned empty page-media arrays for Who and Write. Their exact bodycopies and the public/Freight dependency inventory cover the referenced objects, but the Cargo media-array omission is retained as a source limitation.
- The Freight manifest covers objects referenced by the captured published bodycopies. It is not an inventory of unreferenced files in Cargo's library and does not yet assign ownership, license, or original creative-source provenance.
- Public screenshots and console logs use bundled Chromium at representative desktop/mobile viewports. They are not physical-iPhone, Safari, assistive-technology, performance, or cross-browser verdicts.
- The cloud-backed local workspace had substantial FileProvider dataless state before capture. Hashing hydrated some files, so the exact post-capture flags and the earlier observed counts are both recorded. Tracked content remains recoverable from Git; ignored creative/generated sources require separate recovery hardening.
- Active Montran v17 and V7 bundle reproducibility evidence is recorded under `local/`. Phase 1 does not assign severity or approve remediation.
- No WCAG, performance-budget, browser-matrix, security, SEO, design-quality, or content-parity conclusion should be inferred from this baseline. Those are later audit workstreams.
